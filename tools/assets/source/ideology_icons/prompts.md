# Ideology emblems

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Masters retain the original alpha.
Runtime textures and the size comparison are owned by
`python -B -m tools.builders.build_adiscord_ideology_icons`.
Use `--check` before `--apply`, then repeat `--check` to verify the export.
To export one emblem and preview the other installed textures, add
`--only dorian_technocracy` (or another asset ID listed below).

## dorian_technocracy

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one finished political ideology emblem for Doctor Dorian Worx's technocratic directorate in a Hearts of Iron IV fantasy mod. Meaning: competent engineering administration, published evidence, controlled experiments and direct accountability. A bold silver drafting compass with two spread legs over an open ivory engineering ledger, backed by deep navy blue enamel #202647. A broad industrial cogwheel forms the circular outer border, brushed silver steel with restrained brass fasteners. The book pages have only a few abstract fine ruled lines, absolutely no letters, words, numbers or pseudotext. Distinct clear silhouettes legible at 125x125 and 70x70. Front-facing centered symmetrical heraldic game UI badge, lightly worn beveled metal, painted realistic material, crisp high contrast, restrained ornament, no excessive glow. Fill 88 percent of a square 1:1 canvas. Fully transparent background outside the emblem. No crown, no eye, no sword, no atom or atomic orbits, no skull, no watermark, no multiple icon sheet.

## steland_liberationism

Use case: stylized-concept. One finished raster political ideology emblem for a Hearts of Iron IV fantasy mod, legible at 125 and 70 pixels. Single centered front-facing circular dark enamel medallion with restrained metallic border. Painterly worn beveled metal, strong graphic silhouette, high contrast, subtle texture, restrained ornament; like a classic grand strategy political badge. Fill about 88 percent of the square canvas. Transparent background outside the emblem, no background square. No letters, words, numbers, watermark or sheet of multiple icons. Subject: a prominently broken iron chain in the foreground of a simple civic assembly building with three broad columns and a pediment, a small warm sunrise behind it. Meaning: liberation from slavery and the rule of civic institutions over personal decrees. Dark blue enamel and blue-grey #5C8CA8 accents, ivory stone, silver broken chain and restrained gold sunrise. The broken chain must be clear and occupy the lower central half. A sparse silver laurel border. No weapons, no crown, no eye.
