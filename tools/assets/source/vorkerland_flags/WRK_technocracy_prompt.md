## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand.
Asset type: flat national flag texture for the fictional Hearts of Iron IV country WRK under Doctor Dorian Worx.
Input image is the existing flag to redesign. Preserve its recognizable electric blue, black, silver-white palette and blue-black-blue horizontal flag structure. Improve the central scientific emblem to convey radical technological acceleration and the singularity.
Create ONE finished flag, landscape ratio 82:52, full bleed with absolutely no margins or text. Crisp flat vector-like heraldic geometry, not cloth, not a photograph.
The central emblem: a large black circular medallion edged by a bold silver-white broken accelerator ring. Inside, reinterpret the original narrow flask into a sharp symmetrical blue laboratory vessel / vertical reactor spindle holding a bright white diamond singularity core, tapering to an arrow-point below. Keep it simple, bold and intentional. Two short thick silver forward chevrons integrated at the ring's right break suggest acceleration beyond limits; maintain the overall circular silhouette. A few substantial blue geometric speed bands can connect to the black horizontal field, without tiny decoration.
Composition: strong centered emblem occupying about 80 percent of flag height, broad calm saturated blue upper and lower bands and black middle band. Balanced rigorous engineered forms. Solid flat colors only: near-black, electric cobalt blue, cool silver-white. High contrast, no gradients, no glows, no shadows, no textures, no fine lines, no typography, no numbers, no watermarks, no border around the outer flag. It must remain legible at 82x52 and 41x26 pixels. Deliver a polished actual flag artwork, no mockup.
