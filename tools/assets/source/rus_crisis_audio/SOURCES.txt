Khan finale sound sources

All imported sources below use CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The source files are unmodified. SHA-256 checks are in build_rus_crisis_audio.py.
The generated game mix trims/fades, changes pitch, filters and layers these
sounds with original synthesized charge, beam, rumble and wind textures.
Charge and impact are consecutive parts of one normalized mix. The entity
starts the charge at 0 seconds and the impact at 10 seconds with the detonation.

Last Sky - original synthesized epilogue theme
The 180-second ADISCORD_rus_ruined_world cue develops four phrases of a D-minor organ lament,
inharmonic funeral bells and stereo room reflections with synthesized wind
and the credited receiver and distant collapse layers. The score and instruments are defined
in build_rus_crisis_audio.py; no external music recording is used.
