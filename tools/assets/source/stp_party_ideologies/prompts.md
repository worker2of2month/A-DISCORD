# Party ideology emblems

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. PNG masters retain the original alpha.
The existing High Houses emblem remains at `gfx/interface/ideologies/aristocratic_hedonism_group.png`.

Export runtime textures and the size comparison with:
`python -B -m tools.builders.build_adiscord_stp_party_ideologies --apply`
Use `--check` for a read-only comparison.

## radical_hedonism

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one finished raster ideology emblem for a Hearts of Iron IV fantasy total conversion, displayed at just 125x125 pixels. Square 1:1 canvas with fully transparent background outside the emblem. Ornate but clearly readable heraldic metalwork, front-facing symmetrical composition, bold central silhouette, lightly worn gold/silver beveled edges, restrained painted game UI style. One circular dark enamel medallion with a sparse laurel surround, filling 88 percent of the canvas. No lettering, no words, no numbers, no watermark, no sheet of multiple icons. Thick forms with strong contrast; avoid excessive tiny ornament and excessive glow. Central symbol: a single elegant carnival half-mask with two dark eye openings and a small upward flame above its brow, an emblem of radical personal pleasure and artistic liberation. Enamel palette raspberry purple #AA3E87 with dark plum shadows #372647, pale ivory mask, warm gold trim. No crown, no sword.
